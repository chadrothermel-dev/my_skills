# Fees, Promos, and Switching Traps

Check every candidate plan against this list.

## Pricing traps
| Trap | What to check |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Autopay discount** | Advertised price usually assumes autopay, sometimes only with a debit card or bank account (not credit) |
| **Taxes and fees not included** | Big 3 postpaid usually add taxes, regulatory, and admin fees. Some T-Mobile tiers and most prepaid include them. If unknown, estimate 10–20% for postpaid and say it's an estimate |
| **Admin / "regulatory cost recovery" fees** | Per-line fees outside the plan price; have risen repeatedly |
| **Activation / connection fee** | One-time per line (often ~$35 on postpaid); spread over 24 months in the math |
| **Promo price that ends** | "$X for 12 months" or "first 3 months": compare on the post-promo price, show both |
| **Prepay-to-save** | Mint and others need 3–12 months upfront for the low monthly price. Show the upfront total |
| **Price lock fine print** | Locks often cover the plan price only, not fees or perks |

## Device and lock-in traps
| Trap | What to check |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Bill-credit "free" phones** | Credits paid over 24–36 months; leaving early ends credits and the rest of the phone balance comes due |
| **Remaining installment balance** | Paying it off is a switching cost; include it in 24-month cost |
| **Locked phones** | Carriers require phones to be paid off and active for a set time before unlocking. Verizon locks for 60 days after purchase |
| **Switch-and-pay-off offers** | Usually reimbursed as a prepaid card or bill credits, not cash, and capped per line |

## Perk valuation rules
- Value a perk **only** if the user already pays for it or would buy it anyway.
- Use the service's **current standalone price** for the tier included (often the ad-supported tier).
- Perks can change or disappear; note they aren't price-locked unless the carrier says so.

## Data fine print
- **Premium vs. deprioritized data**: after a premium allowance (or on cheaper/MVNO plans), data can slow during congestion.
- **Hotspot**: often a small high-speed allowance, then throttled to very slow speeds.
- **Video**: cheaper plans often cap streaming at SD/480p.
- **International**: "included" may mean slow data in certain countries only.

## Switching mechanics
- Never cancel the old service before the port completes.
- Port-out PIN and account number come from the current carrier's app or website.
- Watch for a final partial-month bill from the old carrier.
- Family plans: confirm the account holder can port every line.
